# Visitor submissions

Visitors send project briefs and register interest in From the Lab.

## Language

**Project brief**: A visitor's description of work they want to discuss with 17th Street Labs.

**Reader registration**: A visitor's request to remember their access to From the Lab in this browser. It does not imply newsletter consent; articles remain publicly readable.

**Newsletter signup**: A visitor's explicit agreement to receive From the Lab emails.

**Telegram delivery**: Telegram's acceptance of a project brief, reader registration, or newsletter signup for the team's configured chat. Acceptance does not establish that a person has read it or that a newsletter email has been sent.
